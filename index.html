Welcome to Child To Child Support Agency

Child To Child Support Agency is an initiative of a group of health medic practitioners drawn from different health departments who came together for a common goal ‘support communities in improving health and fighting poverty’... is an initiative of a group of health medic practitioners drawn from different health departments who came together for a common goal ‘support communities in improving health and fighting poverty’ our aim is to localize health support to the last-mile communities of Kacheliba & North Pokot Sub-County, a fundamental tool to improving child and mother healthcare among other cross-cutting health issues like TB, Malaria, Kal-Azar, HIV/Aids water borne diseases, water sanitation & hygiene(WASH) among other arising health issues within the pastoralist Pokot community. CCCSA promotes advocacy and awareness raising on child and mother health issues, a linkage between health and poverty reduction for sustainable development. In addition, these vulnerable groups also known as last-mile communities by medics need to access health services from both health amenities and mobile health services that will enable to monitor their health progress, counter challenges affecting them and get recommendations that can influence decision making organs relating to health concerns, these also touches on; health development through research, on communicable & non—communicable by focusing on socio-economic and cultural issues, gender disparities, health governance impacting pastoralist communities’. The organization located in Kacheliba Trading Centre, Kacheliba Sub-County, West Pokot County, in North-Rift region of Kenya. The organization, initiated in 2024 and registered in early 2025 marked as ‘Year of Change to Alleviate Human Suffering’ CCCSA shall also address the continued marginalization of pastoralist communities in the region in all spheres of development and also entitled to fight against Gender Based Violence (GBV), Female Genital Mutilation (FGM), support Sexual Reproductive Health Rights (SRHR), advocate for community participation in public policy on health issues as well as climate change effects in relation to health. According to the CCCSA’s policy, special emphasis is given to children under 5 years, pregnant women, women’s suffering from fistula, People Living with Disabilities (PLWD) among other challenges affecting pastoralists’ communities so as to have the capacity towards combating their health issues for instance, disaster risk reduction brought about by climate change thus resilience, adaptation and mitigation. Networking at various levels and policy advocacy are the important areas for CCCSA’s programme implementation approach. The core features of CCCSA Organization are – 1. Partnership, Networking, and collaboration with like-minded organizations 2. Peer-to-peer education on health issues affecting children and mothers’ lives and pastoral community at large 3. Capacity building through Interactive women clubs like Savings, Internal Community (SILC) network on poverty reduction strategies through life skills trainings etc. 4. Inclusion of vulnerable group especially PLWD and People affected by HIV/Aids 5. Health and hygiene promotion through access to Water Sanitation & Hygiene (WASH) information sharing and services 6. Women-led needs-based community activities 7. Use of Artificial Information medical services over women and young mothers in remote areas. .
